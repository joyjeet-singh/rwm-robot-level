# M1: MuJoCo ANYmal D model and environment (2026-11-07)

**Model** (`step2/model/anymal_d.xml`, written by `scripts/s2/build_model.py` from the description's URDF): 13 bodies, free base plus 12 hinges, 57.03 kg. Inertias are summed from the URDF in double precision and diagonalised in numpy, because MuJoCo's own diagonalisation is accurate only to about 1e-6. Collision shapes: a box trunk, capsule thighs and shanks, and sphere feet. Robot shapes touch only the ground. This is a hand-built model, not the data's simulator.

**MuJoCo 3.10 caveat.** With `fusestatic`, its URDF import moves the fused centres of mass: the whole robot's by 1.2 cm, and its inertia by 0.43 kg·m². The unfused import agrees with a direct URDF sum (4e-9), so the model is built without it.

**Motor: PD fallback.** The ANYdrive file failed to download. The fit on training episodes gives Kp 82.6 N·m/rad, Kd 4.93 N·m·s/rad and a delay of 4 physics steps (20 ms), R² 0.93 (`results/s2/pd_fit.json`). Torques only separate delay groups 0–3 (R² 0.85), 4–7, 8–11 (0.67); the smallest of the best is kept. The timing assumes Step 1's pairing: the policy's output from state r is action row r+1. Rerun fairly, with each convention's own previous action, that pairing fits better (R² 0.40 vs 0.30). The clip is Isaac Lab's DC-motor torque–speed limit (120 N·m saturation, 80 N·m limit, 7.5 rad/s).

**Velocity** in the state is the base's centre-of-mass velocity in the base frame, as Isaac Lab 2.1 defines it.

**Tests** (`results/s2/m1_tests.json`): all six pass. (b) The hip-flexion and knee torque signs match the recorded four-feet-down pattern. (d) The base height drifts 0.7 mm over 9 s.

**Probe:** 1.7 s per 20-s episode with the authors' policy.

**For M2:** the 0.005 s time constant falls below MuJoCo's safety floor of 2 × 0.005 s. MuJoCo therefore runs it as 0.01 s (DECISIONS.md).
