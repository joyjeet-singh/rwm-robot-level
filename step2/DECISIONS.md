# Step 2 decisions

## M0
- **ANYdrive URL unverified.** The agent found the file name but not the full URL (Isaac Lab reads the asset root at run time). Conservative choice: `fetch.zsh` tries two guessed NVIDIA public roots and records success or failure; failure is not fatal (M1 uses the PD fallback per the plan). Recommendation: M1 reads `manifest.json` first.
- **Helper package pins** (absl-py, etils, glfw, pyopengl, importlib_resources, zipp) are my knowledge, not checked against PyPI; a failed pin shows in the fetch output and the manifest check. mujoco 3.10.0 is the pin from PyPI.
- **Isaac Lab v3.0.0 flat task uses the Newton backend;** the facts above are used as the task's settings only.
- **Local mean angles** the agent gave are whole-file means, not standing angles; joint order was taken from rwm_repro/src/rwm_data.py instead.

## M1
- **Motor model: PD fallback** (manifest: ANYdrive FAILED). One Kp, Kd pair shared by all 12 joints, as one network serves all 12 in Isaac Lab. Fitted by least squares on training-episode rows with |torque| < 75 N·m. Delay grid 0–11 physics steps. The recorded torques only separate the groups 0–3, 4–7 and 8–11; the smallest delay in the best group (4) is kept.
- **Timing assumption:** row r's torque is the one applied at the last physics step of the step into row r. The policy's output from state r is action row r+1 (Step 1's pairing). A fair rerun, with each convention's own previous action, favoured it (R² 0.40 vs 0.30).
- **Collision shapes:** the thigh capsule's radius is half the thigh box's width (0.0465 m), not the 0.07 m of the URDF's knee cylinders. So knees touch the ground slightly later. Drive housings are omitted. The payload is not a collision shape. There is no self-collision. Feet use condim 3 (sliding friction only), not Menagerie's 6.
- **Fall rule:** trunk net contact force > 1 N at any physics step of the policy step. Isaac Lab takes the maximum over its history; its history length is unverified. Contacts are net force > 1 N at the last physics step. Foot contact counts the sphere and the shank tube, which together are the URDF's FOOT link.
- **Placeholder constants** until M2 calibrates them: friction 0.8 (Isaac Lab's robot static friction), time constant 0.02 s (MuJoCo's default), armature 0, damping 0. `mj_env.set_constants` sets them. Friction applies to every shape and to the floor, because MuJoCo takes the larger of the two.
- **Note for M2 (design unchanged):** with MuJoCo's default `refsafe`, a contact time constant below 2 × timestep (0.01 s) is raised to 0.01 s. So the grid points 0.005 and 0.01 give identical physics. Recommendation: keep refsafe on (stable), evaluate the grid as fixed, and break the resulting tie by the plan's rule. If still tied, take the larger value, 0.01.
- **Subagent use:** air-bib-checker also fetched Isaac Lab's actuator and articulation source (quotes in `step2/notes/M1_sources.md`), beyond the Menagerie model the block names.

## M2
- **Replay start state** (plan: height from leg kinematics, lowest foot on the ground; orientation from gravity). Yaw is unobservable and set to 0. "On the ground" means the lowest foot sphere just touches it, with no penetration. The motor's delay buffer is filled with the targets of the previous recorded joint commands. The recorded torque is copied into the state (it is an output and has no effect on the dynamics).
- **Windows:** each start is replayed once, for its longest evalh window. Shorter windows are its first h steps. MuJoCo is deterministic, and a test checks that the prefixes are equal. Positions and averaging are evalh's (tested against `evalh.window_floor`).
- **Grid points are evaluated from compiled model text, not from `set_constants` at run time.** Setting armature at run time leaves the compiler's derived quantities stale (`dof_invweight0`, `body_invweight0`, `dof_M0`, `actuator_acc0`). These enter the contact solver. The text evaluated for each point is the same text `--freeze` writes, and its hash is checked. Later sessions must use `step2/model/anymal_d_cal.xml`, not `MjEnv(constants=...)`.
- **Calibrated model file** is M1's XML with the defaults and joint attributes edited. `MjSpec.to_xml` rounds every number to 6 digits, which would undo M1's inertia precision.
- **Exact ties** (0.005 and 0.01 s run identically under refsafe): after E8 and E1, the larger time constant wins, then the earlier grid point (M1's recommendation).
- **Gait statistics:** rows 1 to the end, or to the fall; per-episode means, then the mean over episodes.
  - two feet = exactly two of four feet in contact;
  - duty = contact share, averaged over the four feet;
  - speed error = |(vx, vy) − command| in the base frame; turn error = |wz − command|.
  - The velocity command is Step 0's regime means (it is not recorded). Episode 0 has 999 rows, so its last command is held.
- **Evaluation starts:** `default_rng([2027, e, j])`; all 12 joints get a uniform ±0.05 rad offset; heading is uniform on (−π, π).
- **Refusals:** held-out replays (calibrated *and* uncalibrated) and every policy run are refused until `calibration.json` is frozen and its XML hash matches.
- **Extra, descriptive:** (b) also runs on the uncalibrated model, to help a diagnosis if Gate 1 fails. Gate 1 reads the calibrated run only.

## M3
- **Gate 1 passed** on the calibrated model (G1 1.25%, G2 gap 5.5 points, G3 ratio 0.95); see `step2/notes/M3.md`.
- **Released model's fold:** the plan says `--holdout` comes from the simulator's fold. The released model was trained on all ten episodes (`models.json` arena "all"), so its pilot policy gets no holdout; F18 members get `1,8`. Literal reading of the plan; the evaluation uses all ten schedules for every policy either way.
- **Compressed simulators:** `run_lite.pick_model` ignores the `variant` field and would train in the uncompressed parent. `train_policy.py` builds the model itself (common.load, then compress.apply) and hands it to run_lite; the hash and variant counts are checked against models.json. run_lite is not edited.
- **Imagined return:** the mean of the lite log's `Train/mean_reward` over the last 25 iterations (the plan says only "from the last iterations"). The mean step reward over the same iterations is kept too.
- **Interrupted training restarts from iteration 0** (no mid-run resume), so every policy is one seeded, uninterrupted run.
- **95% intervals** (for P3): percentile bootstrap of the 80-episode mean, resampling the eight starts within each schedule, 10,000 draws, seed 2027. Per-episode values are kept, so M4 can check them another way.
- **Progress:** every 25 iterations as the plan says, and also after any iteration ending more than 5 minutes after the last write, to keep the 10-minute rule on a slow machine.
- **Pilot parallelism:** two training workers of two threads each (4 cores, no session running), then four single-thread evaluations.
- **Pilot teacher-forced model:** B_s0 (seed 0, the plan's "one teacher-forced model").

## M4
- **Gate 2:** P1 passed (released-model policy 10.0% falls < 25%); P2 failed (A_s0, B_s0, A_s0__u50 policies fell in all 80 episodes); P3 passed (the released model's return interval overlaps none of the others). By the plan's rule the **fallback pool** is registered: REL and its eight compressed copies (compress.VARIANTS), two seeds each. Not BLOCKED: P1 passed, and the fallback is the plan's own rule.
- **Fallback checks were not stored.** The M4 block says the checks are stored values, but REL's compressed copies were never built in earlier rounds. Options: (a) stop BLOCKED; (b) compute them now, before registration and before any pool policy exists, with the registered functions only (compute.build/per_model, s1_rollout.score_gru). Chose (b): it changes no fixed design item, uses no physics number, and fixes the values (sha256 in s2_stats.py) before registration. `scripts/s2/fallback_checks.py` -> `results/s2/checks.json`. Arena "all" (20 trajectories), REL's own arena for its stored values; in-sample for all nine (REL was trained on all ten episodes) — a limitation to state.
- **REL recomputed with the copies** (one code path). Recomputed vs stored: E_h and λ1 within 5e-7 relative, λ1_pol and r̄J_pol exact, r̄J 0.13% (stored model_table value 3.2827, recomputed 3.2786 = S1's own recomputation). The recomputed values are used for all nine.
- **Seeds 1 and 2** (plan: "two seeds"), so the pilot's measured REL__p0 is not a result.
- **One training route for all nine:** common.load + compress + lite adapter (`train_policy.py --route rebuilt`); the authors' loader cannot take compressed copies. Adapter = upstream ensemble to 1e-6 (scripts/lite/test_lite.py test 2). The pilot's `--route auto` behaviour is unchanged.
- **train_policy.py fix:** for int8w, models.json records `max_scale`, but the raw compress info has `scales`; the comparison would have failed. It now trims the info as compute.build does. Pilot policies were not affected (no int8w in the pilot).
- **Secondary definitions not in the plan:** fall-rate outcome used as −(fall rate); selection = shortfall of each check's top three against the best three, with SEL1/SEL2 differences; an outcome with no spread is NOT COMPUTABLE; the rollout-trained subset is NOT APPLICABLE (only REL is itself rollout-trained); missing policies: a simulator's score uses its measured policies, one with none is dropped.
- **Imagined return** (secondary ρ): each policy's training-time value (mean Train/mean_reward, last 25 iterations), averaged over the two seeds.
- **Statistical review (air-stats-reviewer), all MUST/SHOULD fixed before registration:** prereg's opening wrongly said no pool-simulator policy existed (REL__p0 does; now stated); pilot imagined returns listed under "what was known"; selection ties by pool position via lexsort, duplicates in a draw are separate entries; NOT COMPUTABLE also when a check is all tied, the outcome has < 3 distinct values, or > 10% of draws are undefined, with dropped draws printed; missing imagined returns drop the simulator from those analyses (not ranked worst); a policy failed twice with a non-finite error is scored falls 1 and the lowest measured return (`results/s2/failed/`), other failures stop the job. NICE items also done (selection n < 6 stated; s2_main checks the checks.json hash; REL/fp16 near-tie stated).
- **Main job parallelism:** probe (session, 2 threads total, 12 iterations, 4,096 robots): one policy × 2 threads 8.85 s/iteration (0.113 it/s); two policies × 1 thread side by side 15.6 and 15.2 s/iteration (0.130 it/s, ~15% more). The pilot's two workers × 2 threads gave 15.8 and 12.9 s/iteration. Chosen: **two workers × 2 threads** (a 4-thread single run could not be probed within the session's 2-thread limit; side by side won at the scale that could).
